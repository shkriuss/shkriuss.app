# shkriuss.app

Open-source Android apps that keep your data on your phone.

**Early development.** The apps are not ready for daily use yet. The
[apps page](../docs/user/apps.md) shows how far each one is.

## What the apps are

- **Local-first.** Every app works fully with no account and no network.
- **No Google services.** No Google Play services, Firebase, analytics, ads
  or trackers. No WebView.
- **No network in data apps.** A separate Sync app does all networking.
- **Optional sync.** End-to-end encrypted sync through a server that you
  run in your own Cloudflare account. The server only sees encrypted data.
- **GrapheneOS first.** Other Android systems come second. Android 13 or
  newer.
