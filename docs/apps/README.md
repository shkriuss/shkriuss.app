# Apps

One folder per app, for the documents that belong to that app alone and
are written for development: its specification. Guides for people who
use the apps are in [user/](../user/README.md). Catalog, the design
gallery, has no specification: what each version changed is in its
[changelog](../../android/app/catalog/CHANGELOG.md).

Folders:

- [water/](water/README.md): Water, the pilot app, with its
  specification.
- [reminders/](reminders/README.md): Reminders, the second app, with
  its specification.
- [tasks/](tasks/README.md): Tasks, the third app, with its
  specification.
- [notes/](notes/README.md): Notes, with its specification. Not built
  yet.

What else belongs here:

- A folder for each further app, with its specification (the
  specification prompt in
  [PROMPTS.md](../development/PROMPTS.md#1-specification)).
